Recipes

Taco Night Bar

Category
Mexican
Protein
Ground beef
Time
25 min
Kids can help
Mash the avocado, tear lettuce, set out the taco bar
Batch
Ground beef, 3 lb (shared with Spaghetti Squash with Meat Sauce)

Ingredients: Ground beef (85/15) (1.5 lb), Taco seasoning (1 jar), Soft taco tortillas (1 pack), Shredded Mexican cheese (1 bag), Avocados (2 each), Salsa (1 jar), Romaine lettuce (1 head), Limes (2 each), Hot sauce (1 bottle). Salt and pepper to taste.

  1. Brown the beefCook in a big skillet, breaking it up, 8 to 10 min. Drain. If batch cooking, set the plain half aside now.
  2. SeasonStir taco seasoning and a splash of water into the taco half. Simmer 3 min.
  3. Kids: toppingsMash avocado with lime and a pinch of salt; tear lettuce into small pieces.
  4. Warm tortillasDry skillet 30 sec per side, or microwave wrapped in a damp towel.
  5. Build your ownSet everything out; everyone builds their own. Hot sauce for the grown-ups.

Tri-Tip Fajita Tacos

Category
Mexican
Protein
Tri-tip
Time
20 min
Kids can help
Pull seeds from peppers, squeeze limes, fill tortillas
Batch
Tri-tip, one 3 lb roast (shared with Tri-Tip, Sweet Potato Wedges & Green Beans)

Ingredients: Tri-tip roast (1.5 lb), Bell peppers (mixed colors) (3 each), Yellow onion (1 each), Soft taco tortillas (1 pack), Taco seasoning (1 jar), Avocados (2 each), Salsa (1 jar), Shredded Mexican cheese (1 bag), Limes (2 each). Salt and pepper to taste.

  1. Prep veggiesSlice peppers and onion into strips. Kids pull out pepper seeds.
  2. SautéCook in a little oil with taco seasoning, 6 to 8 min until soft.
  3. Add the steakSlice leftover tri-tip thin across the grain; warm in the pan 2 min. Starting fresh? Sear and roast it as on Steak Night first.
  4. Build tacosWarm tortillas and fill with steak, peppers, avocado, salsa, cheese, and a squeeze of lime.

Shrimp Tacos with Avocado Salsa

Category
Mexican
Protein
Shrimp
Time
15 min
Kids can help
Pull off shrimp tails, stir the avocado salsa
Batch
Shrimp, 2 lb (shared with Shrimp & Salmon Poke-Style Bowls)

Ingredients: Cooked peeled shrimp (cocktail size) (1 lb), Soft taco tortillas (1 pack), Avocados (2 each), Salsa (1 jar), Limes (2 each), Romaine lettuce (1 head), Taco seasoning (1 jar), Hot sauce (1 bottle). Salt and pepper to taste.

  1. Ready the shrimpShrimp is already cooked. Pat dry; kids pull off the tails.
  2. Season and warmToss with taco seasoning and lime; warm in a skillet 1 to 2 min, or serve cold.
  3. Kids: avocado salsaStir diced avocado into salsa with a big spoon.
  4. Build tacosWarm tortillas; fill with shredded lettuce, shrimp, and avocado salsa.

Spaghetti Squash with Meat Sauce

Category
Italian
Protein
Ground beef
Time
45 min (mostly oven)
Kids can help
Scoop squash seeds, fluff strands with a fork, sprinkle parmesan
Batch
Ground beef, 3 lb (shared with Taco Night Bar)

Ingredients: Ground beef (85/15) (1.5 lb), Spaghetti squash (2 each), Marinara sauce (24 oz) (1 jar), Grated parmesan (1 tub), Garlic (1 head), Salad kit or mixed greens (1 bag), Olive oil (1 bottle). Salt and pepper to taste.

  1. Roast the squashHalve, kids scoop seeds, rub with oil and salt. Roast cut side down at 400°F for 35 to 40 min.
  2. Make the sauceWarm the saved beef (or brown 1.5 lb fresh) with minced garlic. Add marinara; simmer 10 min.
  3. Kids: fluffOnce cooled a bit, kids fluff the squash into strands with a fork.
  4. ServeTop with sauce and parmesan; salad on the side.

Time

  1. Heat the meatballsWarm per the package. Mama Mancini's come in sauce; for Aidells chicken meatballs, simmer them in marinara 10 min.
  2. Kids: cheeseKids sprinkle mozzarella on top; cover a minute to melt.

  4. ServePlate meatballs with sauce, mac, and broccoli.

Italian Chicken Thighs, Broccoli & Mac and Cheese

Category
Italian
Protein
Chicken thighs
Time
35 min
Kids can help
Sprinkle the seasoning, put broccoli on the pan
Batch
Chicken thighs, 4 lb (shared with Korean BBQ Chicken Sticky Rice Bowls)

Ingredients: Boneless chicken thighs (2 lb), Italian seasoning (1 jar), Olive oil (1 bottle), Garlic (1 head), Grated parmesan (1 tub), Broccoli crowns (1.5 lb), Mac & cheese (Costco) (1 pack), Fruit for sides (berries, grapes, mango) (1 lb). Salt and pepper to taste.

  1. SeasonHeat oven to 425°F. Toss thighs with oil, salt, and garlic. Kids sprinkle Italian seasoning on the dinner half.
  2. BakeSheet pan, 25 min, until 175°F. Kids add broccoli to the pan for the last 15 min.
  3. SidesHeat the mac and cheese per its package.
  4. FinishSprinkle parmesan over chicken and broccoli; fruit on the side.

Tri-Tip, Sweet Potato Wedges & Green Beans

Category
Steak & potatoes
Protein
Tri-tip
Time
50 min
Kids can help
Toss sweet potato wedges, snap green bean ends
Batch
Tri-tip, one 3 lb roast (shared with Tri-Tip Fajita Tacos)

Ingredients: Tri-tip roast (1.5 lb), Sweet potatoes (3 each), Green beans (1 lb), Butter (1 lb), Olive oil (1 bottle), Garlic (1 head). Salt and pepper to taste.

  1. WedgesHeat oven to 425°F. Kids toss sweet potato wedges with oil and salt. Roast 30 to 35 min.
  2. Tri-tipSalt well. Sear in a hot oven-safe pan 3 min per side, then roast at 425°F to 130°F for medium-rare (about 20 to 30 min for a whole roast).
  3. RestRest 10 min. Kids snap green bean ends meanwhile.
  4. Green beansSauté in butter and garlic 6 to 8 min.
  5. SliceSlice across the grain. Batch cooking? Refrigerate half for Fajita Tacos.

Steak Night with Smashed Potatoes & Broccoli

Category
Steak & potatoes
Protein
Ribeye, strip, or tenderloin
Time
45 min
Kids can help
Smash the potatoes with a cup, brush on butter

Ingredients: Ribeye, strip, or tenderloin steaks (2 lb), Baby gold potatoes (2 lb), Broccoli crowns (1 lb), Butter (1 lb), Garlic (1 head), Salad kit or mixed greens (1 bag). Salt and pepper to taste.

  1. Boil potatoesSimmer in salted water 15 to 20 min until fork-tender. Drain.
  2. Kids: smashOn a sheet pan, kids press each potato flat with the bottom of a cup and brush on butter. Roast at 425°F for 20 min.
  3. SteakPat dry, salt, sear in a hot cast-iron pan 4 to 5 min per side to 130°F. Rest 5 min.
  4. Broccoli and serveRoast or steam broccoli. Slice steak into kid-size strips.

Korean BBQ Chicken Sticky Rice Bowls

Category
Rice bowls
Protein
Chicken thighs
Time
30 min
Kids can help
Crack eggs, slice cucumber with a kid-safe knife, sprinkle sesame
Batch
Chicken thighs, 4 lb (shared with Italian Chicken Thighs, Broccoli & Mac and Cheese)

Ingredients: Boneless chicken thighs (2 lb), Sushi rice (sticky rice) (1 lb), Korean BBQ (bulgogi) sauce (1 bottle), Cucumbers (1 each), Shredded carrots (1 bag), Eggs (4 each), Sesame seeds (1 jar), Green onions (1 bunch), Roasted seaweed snacks (1 pack). Salt and pepper to taste.

  1. RiceCook sticky rice in a rice cooker, or simmer covered with 1.25 parts water for 18 min, then rest 10 min.
  2. Glaze the chickenChop the saved chicken and glaze in a skillet with Korean BBQ sauce, 3 to 4 min. Starting fresh? Cook thighs 6 min per side first.
  3. EggsKids crack eggs; fry or soft-boil one per person.
  4. Build bowlsRice, chicken, egg, cucumber, carrots. Sprinkle sesame and green onion; seaweed snacks for scooping.

Shrimp & Salmon Poke-Style Bowls

Category
Rice bowls
Protein
Shrimp + salmon or mahi
Time
30 min
Kids can help
Pull shrimp tails, cube avocado with a butter knife
Batch
Shrimp, 2 lb (shared with Shrimp Tacos with Avocado Salsa)

Ingredients: Cooked peeled shrimp (cocktail size) (1 lb), Salmon or mahi fillets (1 lb), Sushi rice (sticky rice) (1 lb), Soy sauce or tamari (1 bottle), Avocados (2 each), Cucumbers (1 each), Shredded carrots (1 bag), Sesame seeds (1 jar), Roasted seaweed snacks (1 pack), Fruit for sides (berries, grapes, mango) (1 lb). Salt and pepper to taste.

  1. RiceCook sticky rice as for the Korean bowls.
  2. FishBrush salmon or mahi with soy and a little oil. Bake at 400°F for 12 to 15 min until it flakes; break into chunks. All seafood is cooked, nothing raw.
  3. Kids: toppingsPull tails off the saved shrimp; cube avocado and cucumber with a butter knife.
  4. Build bowlsRice, fish, shrimp, toppings, a drizzle of soy, sesame. Mango or berries on the side.
